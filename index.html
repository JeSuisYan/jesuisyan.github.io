Watch Library

A personal library of the movies and TV shows you watch, for Windows and Android.

Keep track

Mark titles as Watching, Watched, Watchlist or Dropped, tick the episodes you've seen, and see what's coming next.

Find something to watch

Browse and search movies and shows, with trailers, cast and streaming services, from The Movie Database (TMDB).

The same library on your PC and phone

Optionally, the app keeps your library in sync between your devices through a file in your own Google Drive. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
