My Libraries

Two personal apps, for Windows and Android: Watch Library for movies and TV shows, Game Library for video games.

Watch Library

Mark movies and shows as Watching, Watched, Watchlist or Dropped, tick the episodes you've seen, and browse titles with trailers, cast and streaming services from The Movie Database (TMDB).

Game Library

Mark games as Playing, Played, Backlog or Dropped, see what's coming out, and browse games with trailers, time to beat and series from IGDB.

The same library on your PC and phone

Optionally, each app keeps its library in sync between your devices through a file in your own Google Drive. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Privacy policy · Watch Library uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Game data comes from IGDB.
