Privacy policy

Watch Library · Effective October 8, 2026

Watch Library is a personal app for keeping a library of the movies and TV shows you watch, on Windows and Android. It has no servers of its own: it collects nothing about you, and has no accounts, analytics or ads.

Your library

The titles you add, their statuses, dates and watched episodes are saved on your own device. They are not sent to the app's developer or to anyone else.

The Movie Database (TMDB)

To show movies and shows, the app requests their details from TMDB using your own TMDB API key, which is kept on your device. These requests include what you search for and the titles you open. TMDB's handling of them is covered by TMDB's privacy policy.

Google Drive (optional sync)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app uses it only to keep your library the same on your devices:

  • It asks for access to the files it creates in your Drive (the drive.file permission), and cannot see your other files.
  • It creates one folder, Watch Library, with one file holding your library, and reads and updates that file.
  • It reads your Google account's email address only to show which account is connected.
  • The sign-in keeps an access key on your device. Nothing from your Drive is sent anywhere other than your own devices.

Watch Library's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect at any time in the app's Settings, or remove its access in your Google account's permissions. To delete the synced copy, delete the Watch Library folder from your Drive.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the app removes the library saved on that device. The copy in your Google Drive, if any, stays until you delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
