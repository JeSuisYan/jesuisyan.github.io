Privacy policy

My Libraries (Watch Library and Game Library) · Effective October 8, 2026

Watch Library (movies and TV shows) and Game Library (video games) are personal apps for keeping a library of what you watch and play, on Windows and Android. They have no servers of their own: they collect nothing about you, and have no accounts, analytics or ads. This policy covers both.

Your library

The titles and games you add, their statuses, dates and watched episodes are saved on your own device. They are not sent to the apps' developer or to anyone else.

The Movie Database (TMDB) and IGDB

To show movies and shows, Watch Library requests their details from TMDB using your own TMDB API key, kept on your device; TMDB's privacy policy covers those requests. To show games, Game Library requests their details from IGDB, a Twitch service, using your own Twitch Client ID and Secret, kept on your device; Twitch's privacy notice covers those requests. These requests include what you search for and the titles or games you open.

Google Drive (optional sync)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, each app uses it only to keep its library the same on your devices:

  • It asks for access to the files it creates in your Drive (the drive.file permission), and cannot see your other files.
  • Each app creates one folder, Watch Library or Game Library, with one file holding its library, and reads and updates that file.
  • It reads your Google account's email address only to show which account is connected.
  • The sign-in keeps an access key on your device. Nothing from your Drive is sent anywhere other than your own devices.

The apps' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect at any time in an app's Settings, or remove access in your Google account's permissions. To delete the synced copies, delete the Watch Library and Game Library folders from your Drive.

Removing your data

Uninstalling an app removes the library saved on that device. The copy in your Google Drive, if any, stays until you delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
